# Verdant world wallpaper

`verdant-world.png` is original artwork generated for this project with the built-in image generation tool. It replaces the ornate medieval forest frame in the active interface. The older `forest-frame.png` is retained as a previous asset, not used at runtime.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: bright cinematic contemporary fantasy RPG wallpaper, wide landscape 16:9. A breathtaking enchanted woodland in clear soft morning DAYLIGHT, fresh vibrant green ferns and luminous jade leaves, enormous elegant trees, pale natural standing stones carved with subtle abstract glowing teal runes, distant cascading waterfalls and a sunlit misty emerald valley. Warm sunbeams through light canopy, turquoise water glimpses, spacious inviting exploration and magical discovery mood. High-end current-generation 3D game environmental key art, physically believable foliage and rock, elegant art direction, atmospheric depth, refined rich natural colors, visibly well exposed BRIGHT midtones. Gentle tiny golden motes. Composition: trees, foliage, runic stones mostly on outer left and right edges, open lower center meadow quiet and muted for a separately rendered gameplay overlay; beautiful continuous environment to every edge that tolerates cropping. Bright ivory sunlight and verdant emerald with subtle gold, no black shadows, no night, no gloomy dark fantasy, no castles, no gothic arches, no medieval buildings, no armor, no weaponry, no characters, no ornamental frame, no UI border, no text, no maze. Fantasy roleplaying adventure identity, not cartoon, not a vintage painted game.

## Runtime composition

The scene uses a centered cover crop. Both axes scale by the same factor; excess image at the sides or top and bottom is cropped. No resize operation stretches the wallpaper. Maze geometry and control layout are independent of the crop.

The original SVG wayfinder crest combines a leaf, route and compass diamond. The waygate icon uses a magical circular portal rather than medieval architecture. Cinzel remains the quest display face, paired with readable system sans text.
